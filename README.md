TerraQuest is a browser-based, gamified nature exploration web application. It leverages local, on-device computer vision (powered by Transformers.js) to interactively scan and analyze the environment around you—from recognizing botanical foliage like leaves and pinecones to mineral textures.

Users can use their webcam or upload photos to complete outdoor "Quests", unlock achievements/badges, and build up a personalized "Journal" of their discoveries—all while processing data entirely locally in the browser without relying on external APIs.

Key Features:

 Interactive Scanner: Use your webcam or upload images to scan nature items.
 Local Machine Learning: Utilizes @xenova/transformers for 100% local, privacy-first computer vision inference in the browser.
 Gamified Quests: Complete objectives based on your surroundings to earn XP and unique badges (e.g., Botanist, Woodland Scout).
 Discovery Journal: Keep track of your scanned items, completed quests, and earned badges.
 Hands-Free Guidance: Audio overlay for an immersive, eyes-free outdoor experience
